## Sleeper 10-Team Dynasty League Roster & Draft Guide

In a **10-team dynasty league**, the talent pool is naturally more concentrated than in standard 12-team leagues. To keep the waiver wire from being flooded with starting-caliber talent and to incentivize trading, dynasty leagues use **deep starting lineups, large benches, taxi squads, and injured reserve (IR) slots**.

## 1\. How Many Fantasy Players Are Drafted?

Your **startup draft** on Sleeper drafts your entire active roster (Starters + Bench). Taxi squad spots are typically filled after the draft from your drafted rookies or through a separate rookie draft.

-   **Draft Rounds (Startup):** **25 to 28 rounds** (equaling 25–28 total active roster spots per team).

-   **Total Players Drafted League-Wide:** **250 to 280 players** (10 teams × 25–28 players).
    
-   **Total Player Pool Rostered (with Taxi):** Up to **280–330 players**.
    

## 2\. Lineup Minimums (Starting Positions)

Because there are only 10 teams, starting **10 to 11 players** ("Start 10" or "Start 11") in a **Superflex (SF)** format is the industry standard to ensure positional scarcity.

### Standard "Start 10" Superflex Lineup

| Position Slot | Minimum Starters Required | Notes |
|---------------------|---------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------|
| **QB** | **1** | Core starting quarterback. |
| **RB** | **2** | Starting running backs. |
| **WR** | **3** | Starting wide receivers (3 is preferred over 2 in 10-team). |
| **TE** | **1** | Starting tight end. |
| **FLEX (W/R/T)** | **2** | Any combination of RB, WR, or TE. |
| **SUPERFLEX (Q/W/R/T)** | **1** | Flexible spot; almost always used for a 2nd QB. |
| **Kicker / Defense** | **0** | _Strongly discouraged in dynasty_; rarely used. |
| **Total Starters** | **10** | **Minimum required to field a valid weekly team.** |

## 3\. Position Minimums & Maximums (Roster Limits)

Sleeper allows commissioners to set custom limits per position under **Settings > General > Roster Limits**:

# Sleeper 10-Team Dynasty League Roster & Draft Guide

-   **Minimums:** There is no hard requirement to hold a specific number of backups, but you must hold enough players to legally fill your starting slots each week (e.g., at least 2 RBs, 3 WRs, etc.).
    
-   **Maximums:**
    
    -   **Dynasty Standard:** **No Limit / Unlimited.** In dynasty leagues, positional caps (e.g., "max 4 QBs") are rarely used. Unlimited limits allow teams to rebuild, hoard draft value, and trade freely.
        
    -   **Sleeper Settings (if your league opts to restrict):**
        
        -   **QB:** No limit (or max 4–5 in Superflex)
            
        -   **RB:** No limit
            
        -   **WR:** No limit
            
        -   **TE:** No limit
            

## 4\. Bench, Taxi Squad, and IR Sizes

| Roster Section | Recommended Size | Sleeper Range | Purpose & Rules |
|----------------------|------------------|---------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Bench (BN)** | **15 – 18 slots** | 0 – 30+ | Active reserves available for weekly lineup swaps and waivers. |
| **Taxi Squad (TS)** | **3 – 5 slots** | 0 – 10 | "Practice/training squad" exclusively for stashing rookies/young players without counting against the active roster limit. |
| **Injured Reserve (IR)** | **2 – 4 slots** | 0 – 10 | Reserved for players designated as _Out_, _IR_, or _PUP_ so they do not consume active bench space. |

## 5\. How Taxi Squads Work on Sleeper

# Sleeper 10-Team Dynasty League Roster & Draft Guide

-   **Eligibility:** Commissioners can restrict eligibility to **1st-year (rookies only)** or up to **2nd-year players**.
    
-   **Placement Deadline:** Set to lock at the start of the regular season. Players must be placed on Taxi before this deadline.
    
-   **Promotion Rule:** If you activate a player from your Taxi Squad to your active roster during the season, Sleeper locks that slot—the player **cannot be moved back** to Taxi until the following off-season.
    
-   **Drafting:** On Sleeper, startup drafts fill the active roster (Starters + Bench). Once the draft finishes, team owners manually move eligible rookies to their Taxi Squad slots, opening up remaining bench spots for free agency.
