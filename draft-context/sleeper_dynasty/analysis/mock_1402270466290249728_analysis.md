## 📈 Roster Analysis & Grade

### 1. The Core Build: "Contend Now, Dominate Later"
Your first 12 picks represent a masterful balance of immediate point-scoring floors with long-term elite ceilings. **CeeDee Lamb, Drake London, DeVonta Smith, and Michael Pittman** form a WR room that can anchor your team for half a decade. 

### 2. The Superflex QB Strategy
In a 2QB dynasty format (1 QB + 1 SF slot), QBs often fly off the board aggressively. Passing on QB in the first four rounds and still walking away with **Brock Purdy** and **Baker Mayfield** as your weekly starters is exceptional value. 
To hedge against injury, you grabbed a solid development piece early (**Tyler Shough**) and threw massive late-round volume at the position (**Jalen Milroe**, **Garrett Nussmeier**, **Miller Moss**) to see if any hit.

### 3. The Running Back Volley
Drafting **Jahmyr Gibbs** and **Saquon Barkley** immediately gives you elite top-end firepower. Behind them, you executed a perfect "Zero-Fear" backup build. By throwing extreme volume at high-end backup RBs and touted rookies (Corum, Charbonnet, Vidal, Gordon, Wright, Davis), you ensure that if *any* starting RB goes down around the league, you likely own outsized production that you can start or trade for huge hauls. 

### 4. Tight End Youth
While some teams might chase the veteran TE ceiling, landing **Dalton Kincaid** anchoring alongside **Harold Fannin** and younger developmental TEs keeps the age curve flat and extends the competitive window of your roster immensely.

> [!TIP]
> **Key Takeaway**
> With 34 deep roster spots, treating the last ~10 rounds entirely as a youth developmental dart throw was incredibly savvy. Snagging players like Ollie Gordon, Jalen Milroe, and Jaylen Wright so late ensures your practice squad will have high chances of hitting!

**Final Grade: A+**
