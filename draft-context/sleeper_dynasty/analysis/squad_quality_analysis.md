# Sleeper Dynasty Mock Drafts - Squad Quality Analysis

This document analyzes the squad quality across three 10-team Sleeper Dynasty Superflex mock drafts.

## Mock Draft 1: Slot 4 (`1402270466290249728`)
**Core Strategy:** Elite RB/WR Anchor (Gibbs, Lamb, London, Barkley)
- **QBs:** Brock Purdy (5.44), Baker Mayfield (8.77), Tyler Shough, Jalen Milroe, Garrett Nussmeier, Miller Moss.
  - *Quality:* Safe, high-floor QB2s, but lacks an elite top-tier Superflex ceiling. Good youth pipeline.
- **RBs:** Jahmyr Gibbs (1.04), Saquon Barkley (4.37), RJ Harvey, Blake Corum, Zach Charbonnet, Kaytron Allen, Kimani Vidal, Ollie Gordon, Ray Davis, Jaylen Wright.
  - *Quality:* A dominant top-tier duo (Gibbs/Barkley) followed by an aggressive youth/handcuff strategy.
- **WRs:** CeeDee Lamb (2.17), Drake London (3.24), DeVonta Smith (7.64), Michael Pittman (12.117), Chris Bell.
  - *Quality:* Absolutely incredible WR core. Four startable WR1/WR2s.
- **TEs:** Harold Fannin, Dalton Kincaid, AJ Barner, Jack Endries.
  - *Quality:* Kincaid in the 10th round is an absolute steal, providing long-term TE1 stability.

## Mock Draft 2: Slot 10 (`1402207515285983232`)
**Core Strategy:** Hero WR/TE + Elite Young QBs
- **QBs:** Trevor Lawrence (3.30), C.J. Stroud (9.90), Ty Simpson, Cade Klubnik, Tyson Bagent.
  - *Quality:* Lawrence and Stroud anchor a fantastic long-term Superflex core. Getting Stroud in the 9th is massive value.
- **RBs:** Kyren Williams (5.50), Bucky Irving (6.51), Travis Etienne (7.70), Rhamondre Stevenson (11.110), Chuba Hubbard, Alvin Kamara.
  - *Quality:* Very deep, relying on mid-round volume (Williams, Etienne, Stevenson). Excellent mix of youth and veteran producers.
- **WRs:** Amon-Ra St. Brown (1.10), Emeka Egbuka (4.31), Marvin Harrison (8.71), Jalen Coker, Germie Bernard.
  - *Quality:* St. Brown and Harrison provide a bulletproof dynasty foundation. Egbuka adds youth upside.
- **TEs:** Brock Bowers (2.11), Dalton Kincaid (10.91), Max Klare, Oscar Delp.
  - *Quality:* Elite. Bowers and Kincaid give you arguably two of the top 5 dynasty TEs, allowing for tremendous flex/trade flexibility.

## Mock Draft 3: Slot 9 (`1401313383520731136`)
**Core Strategy:** Elite QB/TE/WR Start
- **QBs:** Lamar Jackson (1.09), C.J. Stroud (14.132), Tyler Shough, Drew Allar, Garrett Nussmeier, Riley Leonard.
  - *Quality:* The best QB room of the three drafts. Lamar Jackson and C.J. Stroud form an unstoppable Superflex duo, and Stroud in the 14th round is league-winning value.
- **RBs:** Bucky Irving (6.52), Travis Etienne (8.72), Kaytron Allen, Kaelon Black, Kimani Vidal, Ollie Gordon.
  - *Quality:* The weakest RB room of the three. It relies heavily on Etienne and Irving, prioritizing youth over proven production. 
- **WRs:** Drake London (3.29), Tetairoa McMillan (4.32), Garrett Wilson (5.49), Marvin Harrison (7.69), Davante Adams (10.92), Quentin Johnston, Michael Pittman.
  - *Quality:* A completely stacked, deep WR room. London, Wilson, Harrison, and Adams provide unmatched depth and upside.
- **TEs:** Brock Bowers (2.12), AJ Barner, Juwan Johnson, Eli Raridon.
  - *Quality:* Anchored by Bowers in the 2nd, the TE room is very secure for the foreseeable future.

## Overall Takeaways & Squad Quality
1. **The Turn (Slots 9 & 10) Offers Massive QB Value:** You were able to secure elite franchise QBs (Stroud, Lawrence, Jackson) much later than expected in drafts 2 and 3 compared to slot 4. 
2. **TE Dominance:** Grabbing Brock Bowers at the 1/2 turn or Dalton Kincaid in the double-digit rounds proved to be a highly successful strategy for solidifying a traditionally volatile position.
3. **WR Depth vs. RB Scarcity:** When drafting from the late slots (9/10), the WR rooms ended up absurdly deep (Draft 3 has London/Wilson/Harrison/Adams). However, this came at the expense of top-tier RB talent, meaning you will have to rely on mid-round volume (Etienne/Irving) and rookie breakouts. Draft 1 (Slot 4) was much more balanced with Gibbs/Barkley anchoring the RBs.
